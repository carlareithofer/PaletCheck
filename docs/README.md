# PaletCheck · Contest deliverables

This folder contains everything I submitted for the **AI-Powered Business Challenge 2026** (Start Your StartUP, POLITEHNICA Bucharest, 20 November 2026), besides the web app itself.

All six deliverables were produced in about 3.5 hours, working solo with AI. They are built around the same idea and the same numbers, so they can be read together.

## The brief in one sentence

Tech-Con installed UR20 collaborative robots to palletize buckets at a food factory. The challenge was to build a business that promotes and sells similar robotic cells to other factories in Romania, with a €30,000 promotion budget and a €300,000 net profit target for the year.

## Documents

| File | What it is | Its purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [`strategy.pdf`](strategy.pdf) | One-page strategy | Explains the problem, the chosen segment and decision maker, the solution, how customers are acquired, national scaling and three implementation steps |

## Key numbers used across all documents

| | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Target segment | paint and building-materials producers in Romania (5–15 kg buckets) |
| Manual lifting, example line | ~60 tonnes per day, ~15 tonnes per operator per shift |
| UR20 payload | 25 kg including the gripper, so buckets of at most ~18 kg |
| Margin per robotic cell | €130,000 price − €110,000 direct cost = €20,000 |
| Cells needed for €300,000 net profit | **22** minimum (a naive calculation gives 17, ignoring tax and other costs) |
| Plan | 24 cells, with 2 as a commercial reserve |
| Promotion budget | €30,000 |

## Assumptions

The brief did not give every figure, so some are my own assumptions and are marked as such in the documents: 16% profit tax, €40,000 of other yearly costs, ~7 kg gripper weight, 7 productive hours per shift, about 2.5 cells per customer. Results from the reference installation are test values, not guarantees for other factories.
